## About: 
Examples of data manipulation with modules pandas[^1], numpy[^2], matplotlib[^3] and seaborn[^4].

---

## Content:
##### weather.ipynb
Plotting the temperature over time  for column "dryBulbTemperature_Celsius"

##### domains.ipynb
Creating a pie chart of people's email domains from data of people-1000.csv. 

##### risk.ipynb
The program that simulates 1000 individual battle rounds in the Risk game (3 attackers vs 2 defenders)[^5] and plots the result. One battle round is one shake of the attacker and defender dice. 

##### knock_airport_weather
Creating plots of:
- The temperature
- The mean temperature each day
- The mean temperature for each month
- The wind speed
- The rolling wind speed over 24 hours
- The max wind speed for each day
- The monthly mean of the daily max windspeeds
